[h1]Reference - Steam Frame LEDs[/h1]

The Steam Frame, Steam Frame Wireless Adapter, and Steam Frame controllers use their respective LEDs to communicate what it happening operationally on the respective devices. This page is to help you visually recognize the LED conditions and diagnose what is happening on your devices.

[h3]Button Chords[/h3]

These are button combinations that may help enter various modes
[table noborder=1 equalcells=1]

[tr]

[td][b]Action[/b]
[/td]
[td][b]Frame Status[/b]
[/td]
[td][b]Result[/b]
[/td]

[/tr]

[tr]

[td]Short Press Power Button
[/td]
[td]Device is Off
[/td]
[td]Device turns on and boots up.
[/td]

[/tr]

[tr]

[td]Short Press Power Button
[/td]
[td]Steam is running.
[/td]
[td]Device suspends (goes to sleep)
[/td]

[/tr]

[tr]

[td]Short Press Power Button
[/td]
[td]Device is in Suspended.
[/td]
[td]Device resumes.
[/td]

[/tr]

[tr]

[td]Power button held for 2 seconds
[/td]
[td]Steam is running
[/td]
[td]Power menu pops up to allow the quick selection of desired power action (reboot, suspend, power off)
[/td]

[/tr]

[tr]

[td]Power button pressed for 8 seconds
[/td]
[td]device is on
[/td]
[td]device will power off, if the device is connected to a USB charger cable, it will begin charging and transition to "Off state charging mode"
[/td]

[/tr]

[tr]

[td]Power button and Volume - (held down for 5 seconds)
[/td]
[td]Device is on
[/td]
[td]Reboots the device but saves some logs to in case of a crash. This is useful in the case of some freezes to get additional logs.
[/td]

[/tr]

[tr]

[td]hold power button for 8 seconds , then release and press the aux button until a menu comes up
[/td]
[td]Off
[/td]
[td]Bios menu comes up, used volume - and + buttons to navigate, and aux to select.
[/td]

[/tr]

[/table]
